What it does

Six small things, each built to remove a reason to stop: looking up a word, keeping it, and meeting it again.

⌕

Look up any word

Search a word and get its senses grouped by part of speech, each with a definition and a real example sentence — not a wall of every meaning at once.

✎

Save it in a tap

Keep the word from the same screen you looked it up on. Mark anything you fumbled for later, and it moves to the front of the queue next time.

◈

Guess the word

You see the definition, you produce the word. Recall in the direction you'll actually need it — reaching for a word you mean rather than recognising one you're shown.

▤

Rebuild the sentence

Write the word into a sentence of your own and get it read back with what worked and what didn't. Knowing a definition and using it are different skills.

◔

A nudge at 20:00

One notification a day with five words waiting. The words stay out of the message — a lock screen is no place for vocabulary — and tapping it opens the session already loaded.

⤓

Works offline

Download your vocabulary to the device and browse it with no connection. Install it to the home screen and it opens like any other app.

How it works

01

Find a word

Look it up, read the sense you meant, and save it. It lands in your list with the definition attached.

02

Practice it

Pick a mode and a handful of words. Two minutes of guessing and writing does more than a re-read ever did.

03

Let it return

The evening reminder brings five words back — the ones you marked first, topped up from the rest of your list.
